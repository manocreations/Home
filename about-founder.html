Mr. Manoj Thiruchelvam

ManO CreationS Hover to meet the heart behind the art.
An engineering graduate from Anna University, he dreamed of becoming an entrepreneur even as a child, while others were busy watching cartoons. Back in 10th standard, his curiosity led him into graphic design, and creativity soon became more than just a hobby. Alongside design, he also became a meme creator, blending humor with creativity in his own unique way. While most people were simply sharing memes, he was creating them. Through self-learning, experimentation, and countless creative nights, his passion continued to grow stronger.
In 2016, driven purely by confidence and creativity, he started his own brand, ManO CreationS. Later, in 2018, he designed his own logo for the brand, proving that he didn't just dream about building something — he actively shaped his own identity from scratch. For him, this step symbolized independence, creativity, and ownership of his work. According to him, true growth does not come only from studying textbooks or following traditional paths, but from continuous learning, experimentation, and real-life experiences. He strongly believes that curiosity, creativity, and the willingness to learn independently are the real foundations of success.
After completing Mechanical Engineering, he chose a different path instead of following the usual routine. To understand life better, he worked as a laborer in more than 20 companies across Tamil Nadu, including major industries like Lakshmi Machine Works and Propel Industries. From noisy factory floors to long overtime shifts and physically demanding work, every experience taught him valuable life lessons. While many focused only on earning certificates, he gained real-world experience, learning discipline, patience, teamwork, and resilience along the way.
Even during those demanding years, his creative side never stopped growing. After long working hours, he continued learning, designing, and building ideas, slowly blending his technical knowledge with artistic vision. Today, through ManO CreationS, he transforms creativity into branding and digital design, proving that passion can grow even in the toughest environments. His story is a unique mix of engineering, hard work, and creativity — a reminder that sometimes the strongest brands are built by people who started from the factory floor, not fancy offices.
