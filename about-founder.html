The story behind ManO CreationS

Manoj Thiruchelvam founded ManO CreationS in 2016. His creative journey brings together engineering, hands-on industrial experience and self-taught design.

An early passion for design

An engineering graduate from Anna University, Manoj Thiruchelvam developed an interest in entrepreneurship at a young age. In 10th standard, he began exploring graphic design and meme creation. Through self-learning, experimentation and creative practice, he continued building his design skills.

Founding ManO CreationS

In 2016, he started ManO CreationS as his own creative brand. In 2018, he designed a logo for the brand, shaping its visual identity. His approach emphasizes curiosity, independent learning, experimentation and practical experience.

Learning from industrial experience

After completing Mechanical Engineering, he worked as a laborer in more than 20 companies across Tamil Nadu, including Lakshmi Machine Works and Propel Industries. Factory-floor work and long shifts provided firsthand lessons in discipline, teamwork, resilience and patience.

Creativity and purpose

While working in industry, he continued learning and creating designs outside working hours. Today, as Founder and CEO of ManO CreationS, he combines technical experience and creative interests in branding and digital design.
