About Kiruthika Industries

Founded in 2010 by Mr. Thiruchelvam J, Kiruthika Industries began as a dedicated steel fabrication workshop rooted in craftsmanship and precision. What started as a one-man operation driven by passion has grown into a trusted manufacturer serving homes and businesses across Tamil Nadu.

Our in-house team of skilled fitters, welders, and fabricators brings decades of combined expertise to every product — from custom teapoys and steel furniture to bespoke industrial components. Every piece leaves our factory floor built to last.

We believe premium quality should never come at a premium price. By cutting out middlemen and selling directly from our factory outlet, we pass those savings on to you.
